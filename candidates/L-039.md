## Step 1: human read of the page
Read on the scan by the session agent, 2026-11-14 (not a human; a human read is still owed). The transcription
matches, with one correction on the signs of the row for 17.

Evidence (verbatim, lower third of the page):
`a = 4n+1   A = □ − ax□  |  a = 4n−1   A = □ + ax□`, with `1 + x +` written above the first column;
`5    1+3+1    1+1`
`13   1+7+15+19+15+7+1    1+3+5+5+3+1`
`17   1+9+11 − 5+15+5+11+9+1    1+3+1+3+3+1+3+1`
In the row for 17 only one minus sign (before the first 5) is clearly legible; the other signs look like plus signs.
Above the table: three short arguments in German and Latin on when `(x^a − 1)^n − (x − 1)^n` is divisible by the prime
2n+1 for all x, and `x − 1 ∽ xp − 1 non semper`. Below it: binomial-coefficient arithmetic whose purpose I could not
determine.

Interpretation (mine): A is 1 + x + … + x^(a−1), the cyclotomic polynomial of the prime a, and the rows are the
coefficients of two polynomials C and D with A = C² − a·x·D². That is the identity now named after Aurifeuille,
Le Lasseur and Lucas. It is not the identity of Disquisitiones art. 357 (4A = Y² ∓ aZ²): the factor x is absent
there and the polynomials differ.

## Step 2: maths checked by code
`python3 -I verify/gm48_11r_cyclotomic_square_forms.py` → ALL CHECKS PASS (2026-11-14).
- Rows 5 and 13 satisfy A = C² − a·x·D² exactly as written.
- Row 17 does not with all signs plus. Among all sign patterns of the written magnitudes exactly one works:
  C = 1+9x+11x²−5x³−15x⁴−5x⁵+11x⁶+9x⁷+x⁸, D = 1+3x+x²−3x³−3x⁴+x⁵+3x⁶+x⁷. So the magnitudes on the page are right
  and four minus signs are missing or not legible.
- For a = 5 the page's C is not Y/2 of art. 357.
- The heading for a = 4n−1 is a true statement (tested on 3, 7, 11); the page has no rows for that case.

## Follow-up (2026-11-15): Lucas 1878, Dickson and Bachmann read
- **Lucas, "Sur les formules de Cauchy et de Lejeune-Dirichlet"**, Association française pour l'avancement des
  sciences, Compte rendu de la 7e session (Paris 1878), pp. 164–173, read on the OCR of archive.org `compterendu07asso`.
  Evidence: he credits the form 4X = Y² ∓ pZ² to Gauss (prime exponent) and Dirichlet (square-free exponent), and
  quotes Cauchy (Comptes rendus 1840, p. 180) for exponents 4n and 8n: "le quadruple du premier membre pourra être
  représenté sous la forme quadratique Y² ± nZ²". He then derives X_n = Y₁² − (−1)^((n−1)/2)·n·z·Z₁² from Gauss's form
  and says: "Si l'on remplace z par −z², on obtient la forme quadratique qui correspond à l'énoncé de Cauchy, pour
  l'exposant 4n." His table of this formula has the rows `[1 + 7 + 15 + 19]`, `[1 + 3 + 5]` for 13 and
  `[1 + 9 + 11 − 5 − 15]`, `[1 + 3 + 1 − 3]` for 17, the same numbers as the Gauss page (half of each palindrome).
  The coefficients "ont été calculés par M. H. Le Lasseur".
- **What this changes (my interpretation).** The x-form is, by Lucas's own account, Cauchy's 1840 statement for the
  exponent 4n rewritten. So the idea was in print in 1840, not first in 1878. Cauchy 1840 itself was **not read**;
  I do not know whether he gave explicit polynomials. The page would still be earlier than Cauchy if it is of the
  diary years, but the gap to the first printed statement is about 30 years, not 70, and the content is a rewriting
  of Gauss's own art. 357 that Lucas carries out in half a page. Failure modes 1 and 6 both bite.
- **Dickson, History I** (OCR of archive.org `historyoftheoryo01dick`): "Lucas gave tables due to LeLasseur and
  Aurifeuille of functions … expressed in the form Y² ± pxyZ²". No earlier source named for the tables.
- **Bachmann, "Über Gauss' zahlentheoretische Arbeiten"** (Werke X.2, OCR of archive.org `s2p1werkehrsgvon10gausuoft`),
  section 6 "Kreisteilung": keyword search finds only "die zugehörige Zerlegung 4X = Y² − (−1)^((p−1)/2) pZ²". No
  mention of an x-form or of the pencil leaves. OCR search, not a full read.
- **Werke X.1, whole volume** (616 pages of OCR): no "Lucas", no "Aurifeuille"; the extra leaves of the booklet are
  mentioned only in the Schlussbemerkung.
- **Still not checked:** Werke II (Nachlass on cyclotomy); Cauchy 1840; Legendre's table (Mém. Acad. sci. XI, cited
  by Lucas for n = 37); the hand; the date.
